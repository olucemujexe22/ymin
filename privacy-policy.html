隐私政策

本政策是永铭官网使用条款的组成部分。使用本网站或主动向我们提交信息，即表示您已阅读本政策。对于依法需要单独同意的处理活动，我们将另行征得您的同意。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于由上海永铭电子股份有限公司（以下简称“永铭”或“我们”）运营的官方网站、产品中心、应用中心、设计工具、下载中心、会员与客户服务功能，以及通过网站在线或离线提交的咨询、报价、样品、技术支持和资料申请。

第三方商城、社交媒体、地图或其他独立运营的网站适用其各自的隐私规则，不适用本政策。

2. 我们收集的信息

2.1 您主动提供的信息

  • 联系与身份信息：姓名、公司、部门、职位、邮箱、电话、国家或地区。
  • 业务需求信息：产品类别、系列或料号、应用场景、目标参数、采购或样品需求、项目进度和沟通记录。
  • 设计与技术支持信息：寿命计算条件、3D-CAD 或 SPICE 申请信息、可靠性需求、技术问题以及您上传的图纸、清单或其他附件。
  • 会员与服务记录：登录凭证的加密校验信息、服务使用记录、下载记录、通知订阅选择及账户设置。

2.2 使用网站时自动形成的信息

  • 设备与网络信息，例如 IP 地址、浏览器类型、操作系统、访问时间、访问页面、来源页面和错误日志。
  • 为保持登录状态、记住语言与界面偏好而保存在 Cookie 或浏览器本地存储中的必要信息。

我们不会主动要求您提供身份证件、金融账户、医疗健康等与产品咨询无关的敏感个人信息。请勿在咨询内容或附件中提交不必要的敏感信息。

3. 信息使用目的

  • 响应询价、样品、产品选型、技术支持和售后服务请求。
  • 提供产品资料、合规文件、CAD/SPICE 资源、寿命计算和其他设计工具。
  • 验证请求的合理性和资源使用范围，保障网站、账户、下载资源及知识产权安全。
  • 改进网站内容、产品展示、搜索与用户体验，并分析网站运行质量和访问趋势。
  • 在您选择订阅时，向您发送产品、技术、活动和培训信息；您可以随时退订。
  • 履行法律法规、监管或争议处理所需的义务。

我们仅在具有合法处理基础时处理个人信息，包括取得您的同意、履行您所请求的服务或合同、履行法定义务，以及维护网站安全和合法权益所必需的范围内。

4. Cookie 与本地存储

网站可能使用 Cookie、localStorage 或类似技术保存语言、登录、界面偏好和安全校验状态。此类信息用于保证功能正常运行、减少重复填写并改善访问体验。

如后续启用访问统计、广告或其他非必要第三方工具，我们将在启用前更新本政策，并按适用法律提供必要的选择机制。您也可以通过浏览器设置清除或拒绝 Cookie，但部分登录、下载、语言记忆等功能可能因此受到影响。

5. 信息共享与委托处理

我们不会出售您的个人信息。为实现上述目的，我们可能在必要范围内向以下对象提供或委托处理信息：

  • 提供网站托管、数据库、邮件、客户关系管理、安全防护或技术支持的服务商。
  • 为处理您的选型、样品、报价或技术请求而需要协作的永铭关联企业或经授权的业务伙伴。
  • 根据法律法规、司法或行政机关的合法要求而必须提供的对象。
  • 在重组、合并或资产转让中，为业务连续性而依法接收信息的适格主体。

我们将按照必要性原则控制共享范围，并要求受托方采取相应保护措施，不得将信息用于未经授权的目的。

6. 信息保存与跨境传输

我们仅在实现收集目的所需的期限内保存信息，或按照法律、税务、质量追溯和争议处理要求保存。超过必要期限后，将删除或匿名化处理，法律法规另有规定的除外。

不同国家和地区的官网访问者可能使用跨境网络。如果业务确需将个人信息传输至所在国家或地区以外，我们将按照适用法律完成必要的告知、同意、合同或安全评估程序。

7. 信息安全

我们采取访问控制、身份校验、传输加密、日志审计、权限分级和备份恢复等合理措施保护信息。互联网环境不存在绝对安全，请妥善保管账户凭证，并避免通过公共设备长期保持登录。

如发生个人信息泄露、损毁或丢失，我们将按照适用法律采取补救措施，并在要求时向监管部门和受影响个人进行通知。

8. 您的权利

在适用法律允许的范围内，您可以请求查阅、复制、更正、补充或删除个人信息，撤回同意，注销会员账户，限制或拒绝特定处理，并获取相关说明。

请通过本政策末尾的方式联系我们。为保护信息安全，我们可能需要先核验您的身份。我们将在合理期限内处理请求；如法律允许拒绝或延长处理，我们会说明原因。

9. 未成年人保护

本网站主要面向企业和工程技术人员，不面向未满十四周岁的儿童提供产品或会员服务。我们不会主动收集儿童个人信息。如发现未经监护人同意收集了儿童信息，将依法及时删除。

10. 第三方链接

网站可能链接至第三方商城、社交媒体、地图、电子邮箱或其他外部服务。点击链接后，您将受该第三方的隐私政策和用户协议约束。我们无法控制第三方独立开展的信息处理活动。

11. 政策更新

当网站功能、信息处理方式或法律要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。重大变化将通过页面提示、公告或其他适当方式告知。更新后的政策自页面标注的生效日期起适用。

12. 联系我们

如您对本政策、个人信息处理或权利请求有疑问，请联系上海永铭电子股份有限公司。

This policy forms part of the terms governing use of the YMIN website. By using the website or submitting information to us, you acknowledge this policy. Where applicable law requires separate consent, we will request it separately.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the official website, Product Center, Application Center, design tools, download center, member and customer-service functions, and requests for quotations, samples, technical support or documents submitted online or offline through the website.

Third-party marketplaces, social-media platforms, maps and other independently operated websites are governed by their own privacy policies and are not covered by this policy.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Contact and identity information, such as name, company, department, position, email address, telephone number and country or region.
  • Business information, such as product category, series or part number, application, target parameters, quotation or sample requirements, project status and communication records.
  • Design and support information, including lifetime-calculation inputs, 3D-CAD or SPICE requests, reliability requirements, technical questions and files you upload.
  • Member and service records, including encrypted credential-verification data, usage history, download records, subscription preferences and account settings.

2.2 Information generated through website use

  • Device and network data, such as IP address, browser type, operating system, access time, pages viewed, referring pages and error logs.
  • Necessary information stored in cookies or browser local storage to maintain sign-in status and remember language and interface preferences.

We do not routinely request identity documents, financial accounts, medical information or other sensitive personal information unrelated to product support. Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive information in inquiries or attachments.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to quotation, sample, product-selection, technical-support and after-sales requests.
  • To provide product information, compliance documents, CAD/SPICE resources, lifetime calculations and other design tools.
  • To verify the legitimacy and permitted scope of requests and protect website, account, download and intellectual-property security.
  • To improve website content, product presentation, search and user experience, and to analyze website quality and traffic trends.
  • To send product, technical, event and training information where you have opted in; you may unsubscribe at any time.
  • To meet legal, regulatory or dispute-resolution obligations.

We process personal information only where we have a lawful basis, such as your consent, performance of a requested service or contract, compliance with legal obligations, or protection of website security and legitimate rights.

4. Cookies and local storage

The website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies to maintain language, sign-in, interface and security states. These technologies help core features work, reduce repeated data entry and improve the browsing experience.

If analytics, advertising or other optional third-party tools are introduced, we will update this policy and provide any choice mechanism required by applicable law before enabling them. You may clear or reject cookies through browser settings, although sign-in, downloads, language memory or other features may then be affected.

5. Sharing and processors

We do not sell your personal information. Where necessary, we may disclose or entrust information to:

  • Service providers supporting website hosting, databases, email, customer relationship management, security or technical operations.
  • YMIN affiliates or authorized business partners that need to collaborate on your selection, sample, quotation or technical request.
  • Courts, regulators or public authorities where required by applicable law.
  • A successor entity in a bona fide reorganization, merger or transfer of assets, subject to appropriate protection.

We limit sharing to what is necessary and require processors to apply appropriate safeguards and not use information for unauthorized purposes.

6. Retention and international transfers

We retain information only for the period necessary to achieve the purposes described above or to meet legal, tax, quality-traceability and dispute-resolution requirements. When that period ends, we delete or anonymize the information unless the law requires otherwise.

Visitors from different regions may access the website through international networks. If a transfer of personal information outside its originating jurisdiction is required, we will implement the required notices, consent, contractual safeguards or security assessments under applicable law.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as access controls, authentication, encrypted transmission, audit logs, role-based permissions and backup recovery. No internet environment is completely secure; please protect your credentials and avoid staying signed in on shared devices.

If personal information is breached, destroyed or lost, we will take remedial action and provide notifications to regulators and affected individuals where required by law.

8. Your rights

To the extent provided by applicable law, you may request access to, a copy of, correction of, supplementation of or deletion of your personal information; withdraw consent; close a member account; restrict or object to certain processing; and request an explanation.

Please contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity. We will respond within a reasonable period and explain any lawful reason for refusal or extension.

9. Minors

The website is primarily intended for businesses and engineering professionals and does not offer products or member services to children under 14. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If we learn that such information was collected without guardian consent, we will delete it as required.

10. Third-party links

The website may link to third-party marketplaces, social media, maps, email services or other external services. Once you follow such a link, the third party's privacy policy and user terms apply. We do not control their independent processing activities.

11. Policy updates

We may update this policy when website features, processing practices or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through a website notice, announcement or other appropriate method. The updated policy applies from the effective date shown on this page.

Cookie 政策

本政策说明永铭官网如何使用 Cookie、浏览器本地存储及类似技术，以及您如何管理这些信息。

Cookie 是网站存放在浏览器中的小型文本文件。浏览器本地存储与 Cookie 类似，可用于记住语言、登录状态和界面选择。合理使用这些技术可以帮助网站稳定运行，减少重复操作，并改善访问体验。

1. 什么是 Cookie

Cookie 是您访问网站时由网站或第三方服务写入浏览器的少量数据。它们通常不会单独识别您的真实身份，但可能与您主动提交的账户或联系信息关联。

本政策中的“Cookie”也泛指 localStorage、sessionStorage、像素标签及用途类似的技术。

3. 当前存储项目

当前网站主要使用第一方必要存储，典型项目包括：

  • ymin-language：保存用户选择的页面语言，以便后续访问保持一致。
  • 会话与账户状态：会员登录后用于识别当前会话和安全校验；具体名称可能随正式后台实现调整。
  • 表单和界面偏好：在您主动操作时保存必要的筛选、显示或交互状态。
  • 资源和下载校验：保护受限下载、会员资源和接口请求的安全状态。

具体存储名称、保存期限和技术实现可能随网站功能升级而调整，但用途不会超出本政策所述范围。

4. 第三方服务

当前网站以本站必要功能为主。百度统计、Google Analytics 或其他访问分析工具是否启用，以正式上线配置为准；在启用前，我们会根据适用法律完成告知、同意或匿名化安排。

如未来启用第三方分析或地图、视频、客服等嵌入服务，该服务商可能读取或设置其自身 Cookie，并按照其隐私政策处理信息。我们会在本页面更新服务名称、用途和相应链接。

5. 管理 Cookie

您可以通过浏览器设置查看、删除或阻止 Cookie。常见浏览器的管理入口如下：

  • Chrome：设置 → 隐私和安全 → 第三方 Cookie
  • Edge：设置 → Cookie 和站点权限 → 管理和删除 Cookie
  • Firefox：设置 → 隐私与安全 → Cookie 和网站数据
  • Safari：设置 → 隐私 → 管理网站数据

清除本地存储后，语言选择、登录状态、会员历史和部分表单偏好可能被重置。禁用必要 Cookie 或类似技术可能导致登录、下载、表单提交或会员功能无法正常使用。

6. 同意与撤回

对于网站运行所必需的技术，我们会基于提供您所请求服务的必要范围使用。对于非必要分析或营销技术，我们仅在满足适用告知或取得相应同意后启用。

您可以随时通过浏览器设置撤回或调整选择。若网站后续提供独立的 Cookie 偏好中心，其设置将与本政策共同适用。

7. 政策更新

当 Cookie 类别、第三方服务或法律要求发生变化时，我们会更新并在网站正式发布最新内容。重大变化将通过网站提示或公告说明。

Cookie Policy

This policy explains how the YMIN website uses cookies, browser local storage and similar technologies, and how you can manage them.

On this page

1. What cookies are 2. Types we use 3. Current storage items 4. Third-party services 5. Managing cookies 6. Consent and withdrawal 7. Policy updates 8. Contact us

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser by a website. Browser local storage works in a similar way and may remember language, sign-in and interface choices. Appropriate use of these technologies helps the website operate reliably, reduces repeated actions and improves the browsing experience.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small amounts of data written to your browser by the website or a third-party service when you visit. They usually do not identify you directly, but may be associated with account or contact information you voluntarily submit.

References to “cookies” in this policy also include localStorage, sessionStorage, pixels and technologies with similar functions.

2. Types we use

3. Current storage items

  • ymin-language: remembers the selected website language for future visits.
  • Session and account state: identifies the current member session and supports security checks. Names may change when the production backend is finalized.
  • Form and interface preferences: retain necessary filtering, display or interaction states when you actively use a feature.
  • Resource and download verification: protects restricted downloads, member resources and API request states.

Storage names, retention periods and technical implementation may change as website features evolve, but their use will remain within the purposes described here.

4. Third-party services

The current website focuses on first-party functionality. Whether analytics tools such as Baidu Analytics or Google Analytics are activated will depend on the final production configuration. Before activation, we will implement any required notice, consent or anonymization measures.

If analytics, maps, video, customer-service or other embedded services are added later, their providers may read or set their own cookies under their privacy policies. We will update this page with the relevant services and links.

5. Managing cookies

You can inspect, delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Common entry points include:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Clearing local storage may reset language, sign-in state, member history and some form preferences. Disabling necessary cookies or similar technologies may prevent sign-in, downloads, form submission or member features from working correctly.

6. Consent and withdrawal

We use technologies necessary to provide requested website services. Non-essential analytics or marketing technologies will be enabled only after applicable notice or consent requirements are met.

You may withdraw or adjust your choices through browser settings at any time. If a dedicated cookie preference center is introduced, its settings will apply together with this policy.

7. Policy updates

We will update and publish the latest version of this policy when cookie categories, third-party services or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through a website notice or announcement.
